A question is rarely a sentence. It is usually a region of possibility with a few bright constraints and a much larger dark volume around them.

## Begin with the boundary

Before solving a problem, describe what would count as an answer. This changes the work from wandering through an infinite search space into navigating a finite landscape.

## Keep the unknown visible

Good research interfaces should not hide uncertainty. They should make it spatial: what is close, what is distant, what is bright because evidence supports it, and what is only a faint hypothesis.

The nebula metaphor of this site is not decoration. It is an attempt to make that structure tangible.
